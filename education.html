ADAS Education &
Training Resources

The ADAS industry is evolving rapidly, and staying informed has never been more important.

Through our partnership with Revv and the United ADAS Collective (UAC), we're excited to share access to the newly launched UAC On-Demand Learning Portal—a comprehensive educational resource designed to help collision repair professionals, estimators, technicians, managers, and shop owners build confidence in ADAS operations and workflows.

The platform includes:

  • ADAS Maturity Curve Self-Assessments
  • 80+ online lessons and training modules
  • Short-form educational videos
  • Knowledge checks and quizzes
  • Downloadable resources and reference materials

Whether you're new to ADAS or looking to strengthen your team's expertise, the UAC Learning Portal provides practical, structured education that can be accessed whenever it's convenient.
