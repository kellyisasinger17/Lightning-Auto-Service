Accessibility Policy

Last Updated: 6/15/26

Lightning Auto Service LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to making our website, lightningautoservice.com, accessible and usable for all visitors, including individuals with disabilities. We believe every customer should be able to access information about our services, contact our team, and use our website with ease.

Our website has been reviewed by a third party for accessibility, and we will continue to periodically monitor and improve the site to support accessibility best practices. While we strive to maintain an accessible website, some content may not yet fully meet all accessibility standards at all times.

If you experience any difficulty accessing any part of our website, please contact us so we can assist you and work to address the issue.
