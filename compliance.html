Regulatory Compliance:
State and Federal Requirements

Lightning Auto Service operates in strict alignment with:

  • DOT and NHTSA safety recommendations
  • OEM Calibration Position Statements
  • I-CAR Gold Class standards
  • New Jersey Administrative Code N.J.A.C. 11:3-10.3 and 11:2-17.10(a)
  • California Code of Regulations Title 10, Section 2695.8(f)
  • Texas Administrative Code Title 28, §5.501
  • Florida Statute 626.9743(4) and other state-specific regulations governing safe, fair, and necessary post-collision repairs.

We remain actively engaged in regulatory updates and compliance measures in all 50 states, ensuring our services meet or exceed the repair standards and insurance codes in the jurisdictions we serve.

Lightning Auto Service provides OEM-compliant ADAS calibrations using manufacturer-approved equipment, documented procedures, health scans, and photo verification to help ensure vehicles are safely restored to pre-loss condition while reducing risk for drivers, repairers, and insurers.
